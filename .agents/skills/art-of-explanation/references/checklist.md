# Step Four checklist (C1–C15)

Source: Ros Atkins, *The Art of Explanation*, **Step Four: Organise the
information**. Section headings below match the chapter in `source.md`.

Apply each check to the user's artifact and record: status, evidence
(`file:line` or quote), and the concrete gap.

## A. Make a list of the main strands

- **C1 — Strand list exists.** The artifact contains a list of the main strands
  of the subject. (Section: *Make a list of the main strands of the subject*.)
- **C2 — Short headers.** Each strand has a short header/label.
- **C3 — High-impact strand present.** A strand dedicated to high-impact
  information (elements that might start or finish the explanation) has been
  added. (Section: *Core strands plus two more*.)
- **C4 — Not-sure strand present.** A strand for information the author is
  unsure how to use has been added.
- **C5 — Provisional is allowed.** Record if the list has fewer or more strands
  than the example; there is no fixed number. Mark ✅ if the list exists, even if
  it is expected to change. Do not fail on count or order.

## B. Organising the strands

- **C6 — Core strands ordered.** The core strands have a first-pass ordering
  (C, A, D, … in the chapter). The two added strands stay at the end.
  Provisional ordering is fine — only a first attempt is required.

## C. Add the information

- **C7 — Elements placed.** Distilled elements from the earlier (pre-Step-Four)
  work have been moved into strands. If the distilled list is a separate file,
  read it and confirm placement.
- **C8 — High-impact elements flagged.** Elements judged "clear, relevant and
  high impact" are identified in the high-impact strand (or clearly marked).
- **C9 — Nothing left unplaced.** All the author's distilled information is in
  one of the strands. Verify by comparing the source element list with what
  appears in strands; list anything unaccounted for.

## D. Organise the information in the strands

Applies to the **core strands only**. High-impact and not-sure strands are
deferred and must not yet be internally ordered.

- **C10 — Strand goal stated.** For each core strand, the author records what
  they hope the strand will achieve. (First organising question.)
- **C11 — Elements ordered within each strand.** Each core strand has a
  sequence: a chosen starting element and the elements that follow. (Order need
  not be final.)
- **C12 — Each element's role is clear.** Every included element has an
  identifiable role in that strand's explanation. Flag elements that are listed
  with no discernible purpose.
- **C13 — Deferral respected.** The high-impact and not-sure strands are left
  unordered at this stage. Being unordered is correct; do not flag it. Do flag
  it if the author organised them anyway.

## E. Leftovers

- **C14 — Leftovers moved to not-sure.** After going through each strand, any
  element not used was double-checked and moved into the 'not sure' strand
  rather than discarded. Mark ➖ N/A if there genuinely were no leftovers.

## F. Quick Check

- **C15a — Strands assessed.** The agent may partially assess "Do your strands
  work?" — whether the strand set plausibly covers the stated purpose and story.
  Mark ⚠️ if it cannot be judged from the artifact.
- **C15b — "Things to understand better" list.** There is a list of things the
  author still needs to understand better. ❌ if absent.

### Author-only questions (report as ❓, do not score)

From the chapter's **Quick Check**, these ask for intent or feeling and cannot be
verified from the artifact:

- What is the purpose of your explanation?
- What is the story you want to tell?
- How do you want to tell that story?
- Are you missing any visual elements that you'd like?
- Have you discovered new areas you think you need to cover?
- How do you feel about the information in your 'not sure' strand?
- Do you need advice?
